Privacy Policy Last updated: September 18, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how Gold Hunt 3D handles information when you use the game. Gold Hunt 3D is developed and published by cidstech. 1. Information We Collect Gold Hunt 3D does not require you to create an account, provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal profile information to play. When you use multiplayer features, the game may transmit temporary gameplay information required to connect players and synchronize the game, including a temporary player identifier, room/game code, player position and movement information, player status, and gameplay events such as joining, leaving, movement, and shooting. This multiplayer information is used to provide and synchronize the multiplayer game experience. 2. Third-Party Service Provider Gold Hunt 3D uses Supabase as a backend service for multiplayer communication, including realtime presence and game events. Information transmitted for multiplayer functionality may therefore be processed by Supabase as our service provider. For information about Supabase's privacy and data processing practices, see the Supabase Privacy Policy. 3. Information We Do Not Request Gold Hunt 3D does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, phone calls, or photos/media for the game's core functionality. 4. Advertising Gold Hunt 3D does not currently display advertising or use an advertising SDK. 5. Data Retention Multiplayer information is intended to support active game sessions. Temporary gameplay information is not intended to be maintained as a permanent player profile. We do not require an account for Gold Hunt 3D. 6. Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the game. Multiplayer communication is provided through Supabase's hosted services. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. 7. Children's Privacy Gold Hunt 3D is not designed to require children to submit personal information. The game does not knowingly request personal information such as a child's name, email address, telephone number, or precise location. 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when the game's functionality or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. 9. Contact If you have questions or privacy-related requests concerning Gold Hunt 3D, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Gold Hunt 3D Google Play listing. 10.
